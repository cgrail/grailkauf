Last updated: November 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Grailkauf handles information in the iPhone app, its share extension, its Lock Screen Live Activity, the Apple Watch app, and the Mac app.

Overview

Grailkauf is designed to work without the developer ever receiving your data. It does not require account creation, does not run a server of its own, and does not collect personal data for analytics, marketing, or account management.

Information You Enter

The app stores what you enter to plan your shopping: the items on your list, their sections, the items you ticked and when, the shopping trips made of them, the lists you archived, and the order in which you pick up your items, which the app learns from your ticks.

The app does not ask for email addresses, phone numbers, or account credentials, and it does not create user accounts.

The app does not use cookies, analytics, advertising identifiers, or behavior tracking technologies.

On Your Devices

Everything you enter is stored on your devices first, so the app works without internet. Your settings, such as list or shop mode and the grid view, and the time of the last sync are kept in the app's settings on the device.

Deleting the app removes what it stored on that device.

iCloud Sync

Your list, trips, learned order, and the order of the supermarket sections sync between your devices through your private iCloud database, under your Apple ID.

This data is stored by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy. The developer has no access to your private iCloud database. You can delete the app's data in iCloud from your device's iCloud settings.

Shared List

If you invite another person, your list is shared through iCloud sharing between your Apple ID and theirs. They can see and change the full list, its items, ticks, trips, and learned order, and they see your name as iCloud shows it on the share.

The owner can remove people at any time, and each person can leave the list.

iPhone and Apple Watch

Your iPhone and Apple Watch pass the list, your ticks and the order of the sections directly to each other over Bluetooth, so they work without internet. Nothing in this passes through a server of ours.

Dictation

With your permission, the app uses the microphone while you hold the dictation button, and Apple's speech recognition turns your words into items. The app asks for recognition on the device. Where your iPhone cannot do that, Apple's speech recognition may send the audio to Apple under Apple's terms. The app keeps no recording.

You can change these permissions at any time in your device's settings.

Apple Intelligence

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's on-device model splits entries into single products and guesses where a new item goes in the store and its section. It runs on your iPhone. The app does not send your items anywhere for this.

Lock Screen and Share Sheet

While shop mode is open, the Lock Screen shows your progress and the next items. This stays on your iPhone.

When you share text from another app to Grailkauf, the share extension keeps it on your iPhone until the app adds its lines to your list. The text is not sent anywhere else.

Third-Party Services

The app does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It uses Apple's iCloud, speech recognition, and Apple Intelligence.

If you have chosen in your device's settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports.

Children’s Privacy

Because the developer does not collect or store personal data, the app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any updates will be published on this page.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be directed to the app developer through the contact method provided on the app’s App Store product page.

App Store Privacy Summary

For App Store privacy labeling purposes, this app is intended to operate without collecting data and without tracking users across apps or websites. Your shopping data stays on your devices, in your private iCloud database under your Apple ID, and with the people you choose to share your list with. None of it is accessible to the developer.
