# Changelog

## 1.0 (current)

- You write the shopping list like a chat: type an item, press Enter, type the next item.
- The app suggests items that you added before, and items that are due again.
- "Fill with the usual" adds all due items at once.
- You can dictate many items at once. Hold "Hold to dictate" above the text field, say the items and let go. After each pause, the screen shows the items of what you said, split into single products and without filler words such as "ähm" or "dann noch". A spinner shows while the app splits. A moving waveform shows the words that the phone hears now, a microphone shows while it waits.
- While you hold the dictation button, a bar as in a messaging app shows what the app does, a red waveform of your voice and the time. Rings around the button pulse and grow when you speak.
- A spinner line in list mode shows while the app checks new items.
- If you add an item that is on the list already, the older copy goes away. A short notice tells you so. If you say or type an item twice at once, it goes on the list once.
- Tap an item in list mode to change its text in place, as in a notes app. An emptied item goes away.
- With Apple Intelligence, the app splits an entry such as "Milch Sprudel" into single items. "saure Sahne" stays one item. A new "Paprika" always goes to "Fruit & vegetables".
- Each item in list mode has a trash button.
- You can drag the items in list mode into a new order.
- "Select" in list mode lets you select many items and delete them at once. Slide a finger down the circles to select many items quickly. "Select all" selects the full list. While you select, the other buttons at the top go away.
- The "Sections" tab at the top shows the items as small tiles with a picture, five in a row, grouped by supermarket section such as "Fruit & vegetables". Each section has its own color, as on a store map. The sections follow the route through a typical supermarket. Small sections fill the gaps in the rows above. Each row fills the full width of the screen, so a short list gets larger tiles. Touch and hold a tile to move the item to another section. Items that the app has not sorted yet go to "Other". Type an item below the tiles and press Enter: the app adds it and goes to the list for the next item. The tiles show no suggestions. Shop mode has the grid too, with a button to switch between list and grid: tap a tile to tick the item. Ticked items go to "Done" below a line at the bottom of the grid, grouped by faded sections. Tap "Done" to show or hide them. A tap on a tile there brings the item back.
- The tiles show a picture of the kind of item: a hot dog for sausages such as "Wiener" or "Salami", a turkey for "Putengeschnetzeltes", noodle soup for "Chinanudeln", a pretzel for "Laugenstangen". A note after the item, as in "Zahnseide Papa" or "H-Milch (5€)", does not change the picture.
- The menu at the top left lists your past shopping trips and has the settings at the bottom. Each trip shows the items that are not on the list now, so you see what your list can miss. A line below counts them and the items that are already on the list. Open a trip and tap + to add an item to the list again.
- Shop mode sorts the list in the order in which you pick up your items. The app learns this order from your ticks.
- An item that the app does not know yet goes to the "New" group, or next to items of the same kind.
- The order screen shows the learned order. You can drag an item to correct it.
- You can invite another person to your list. The list syncs through iCloud.
- The Apple Watch app shows the current item on top and the rest of the list below it, grouped by supermarket section. You tick an item with a tap or with Double Tap.
- The iPhone and the Watch sync over Bluetooth, also with no internet.
- On the Watch, the progress is a ring with a count in the top left corner, in the size of the time and level with it, so the current item fits on the first screen. Each open item has an empty circle and a picture, and a ticked item has a check mark. The current item is at the top, below the name of its supermarket section, with no "New" label and no line with the next items.
- "Info" at the end of the list on the Watch opens an info screen with the time of the last sync, the version and the commit of the app. "Refresh" is there now, not at the end of the list.
- When you tick the last item in shop mode, the iPhone and the Watch show a big 🎉 and "All done.".
- The Lock Screen shows the progress of your shop.
- You can share a text from another app, for example a note, to Grailkauf. Each line and each part between commas becomes one item, without checklist marks. The app does not split a shared line into more items: "String cheese" stays one item. After you share, Grailkauf opens on the list.
- The app is in English and German.
- "New list" at the top of the menu starts an empty list. The old list goes to the history as an archived list, and you can add its items again from there. It also ends the shop in progress: the ticked items leave the list, and the app goes to list mode.
- Below the items in list mode, "Suggestions" shows the items of your past trips and lists that are not on the list now. Tap one to add it. The list stays where it is. Swipe one to remove it, for example a thing that you bought once. It comes back if you buy it again.
- In the "Sections" tab, tap a tile to change the name of the item, move it to another section or delete it.
